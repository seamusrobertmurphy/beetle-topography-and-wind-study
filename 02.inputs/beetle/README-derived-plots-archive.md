# Archived derived-plot outputs

Moved aside on 2026-09-02 when the field-plot classifier became the study's response.

`red-stage-darkwoods-derived-plots/`, `lag-covariates-derived-plots/` and
`model-data-derived-plots/` are the outputs of the 1000-plot classifier of chunk 24, whose
training plots were sampled from the imagery's own NDMI drop inside a forest mask that
admitted open water. The live folders of the same names now hold the outputs of chunk 24b,
trained on the parent study's 28 ground plots with undisturbed forest as the unaffected
class and predicted over the perimeter with water masked. Nothing in the manuscript reads
the archived folders. They are kept because the classifier comparison in
`.claude/memory/domain/red-stage-classification.md` cites them.
