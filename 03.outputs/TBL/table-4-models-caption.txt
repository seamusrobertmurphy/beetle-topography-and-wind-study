The annual models, each adding one mechanism, over the nine years without and the eight years with a previous map without and with the dependence terms. M0 held host size, shading and landform, M1 added basal area, M2 terrain exposure, shape and flight-window radiation, and M3 the interactions of basal area with wind shelter, July wind and radiation. Cross-validation (CV) used ten folds of 1 km blocks, and the blind test fitted the years to 2012 and predicted 2013 and 2014. The RMSE ratio was the test-fold over the training-fold root mean square error, and Theil's U bias the share of the mean squared error due to the difference of the means.
