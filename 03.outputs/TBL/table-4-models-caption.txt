The annual models, each adding one mechanism to the one before, over all nine years without the dependence terms and over the eight years with a previous map without and with them. M0 held host size, shading and landform, M1 added basal area, M2 terrain exposure, terrain shape and flight-window radiation, and M3 the interactions of basal area with wind shelter, July wind and flight-window radiation. AUC was the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve and Brier skill the reduction in mean squared error of the fitted probabilities against the base rate.
