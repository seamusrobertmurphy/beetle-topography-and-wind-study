Agreement of the red-stage classifier and the terrain wind with independent measurements. The classifier was scored over 100 held-out splits, and wind speed over the flight hours of June to August 2005 to 2014 at four valley stations, corrected as in the models. Bias was the predicted less the measured mean speed in km/h, RMSE the root mean square error, Theil's U bias the share of the mean squared error due to that difference, and direction error the mean absolute difference in degrees.
